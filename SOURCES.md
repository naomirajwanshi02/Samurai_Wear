# Sources

## The armour

The suit is the **Armor (Gusoku)**, about 1650, in the Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York:
accession 36.25.41a–q, Bequest of George C. Stone, 1935; helmet signed by Saotome Ietada; materials
iron, leather, lacquer, silk, textile, hair, copper alloy; Gallery 378.
Record: https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/32100 (read through the Met's Open Access API,
https://collectionapi.metmuseum.org/public/collection/v1/objects/32100, on 14 November 2026).

The record has **no photograph and no description**, so the shape, colours and decoration of every part
follow the reference drawing of this armour supplied for the project (`assets/refs/gusoku-1650-ref.jpg`).
Where the drawing is unclear (the helmet bowl, hidden behind the crest; the throat guard, hidden by the
cords), the model uses a plain form typical of Edo-period armour and says so in `js/armour.js`.

The Met's record lists Saotome Ietada as active in the early to mid-19th century while dating the armour to
about 1650, so the signed helmet may be later than the rest of the armour; the app says only that the
helmet is signed by him.
