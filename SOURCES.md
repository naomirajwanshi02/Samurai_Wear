**Simplifications.** Counts follow the sources where they agree (five-lame neck guard, four turned back,
seven-lame sleeves, four-panel five-lame skirt, three-lame sendan-no-ita). The bowl's 16 plates and the
egawa pattern are representative, not copied from one helmet. The happuri's exact outline varied; it is
drawn here from the general description (forehead and both cheeks).
